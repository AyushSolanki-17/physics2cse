This path follows one question: how can physical matter become a machine that prints `Hello, world`?

The answer is a chain of constraints and design decisions. The first five steps are complete enough to study now.

## How to Use This Path

Do not memorize the list. At each step, ask:

- What limitation forced this concept to exist?
- What can now be measured or controlled?
- What becomes possible next?

When a later page is added, it should connect back to the concept that made it necessary.
